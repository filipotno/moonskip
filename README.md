# Moon Skip
Switch rings, dodge asteroids, and collect gold around six planetary worlds.

**[Download Moon Skip 1.6 for Android](https://github.com/filipotno/moonskip/raw/refs/heads/main/docs/downloads/Moon-Skip-1.6.apk)** · [Website](https://filipotno.github.io/moonskip/)

Open the APK on an Android 7.0+ device and follow the installer. Install over 1.4 or 1.5 to update.

In solo flight, collect gold for points and coins. Eight coins buy a five-second launch shield in Shop & hangar. A red orb grants a ten-second shield during flight.

Choose **Orbit duel** to play with a friend on one phone. Each pilot has two steering buttons. Fly into the gap in each of 16 gates. Whoever clears more gates wins.

Version 1.6 opens in landscape. Survive 24 seconds to unlock the next sector checkpoint. Meteor storm adds rocks and gold, and doubles coin payouts. A permanent fast laser costs 30 coins.
