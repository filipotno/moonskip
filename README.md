# Moon Skip

Switch rings, dodge asteroids, and collect gold around six planetary worlds.

**[Download Moon Skip 1.7 for Android](https://github.com/filipotno/moonskip/raw/refs/heads/main/docs/downloads/Moon-Skip-1.7.apk)** · [Website](https://filipotno.github.io/moonskip/)

Open the APK on an Android 7.0+ device and follow the installer. Install over your previous version to keep your wallet and checkpoint.

**Solo flight.** Survive 24 seconds to reach the next sector. Watch for the opening as traffic moves between rings. Switch on Meteor storm for tighter spacing and more gold. A rare red Nova gives you ten seconds of shielding.

**Local duel.** Share one phone in landscape. Mint and Coral start opposite each other on the same ring. Each pilot has an inward and an outward arrow. Both face matching hazards. Survive with more lives after four laps, or win when the other ship loses its last life.

**Ship shop.** Open Shop from the hangar to change hull and color, buy a five-second launch shield for eight coins, or install a permanent faster laser for thirty. Open Settings to adjust steering, engine trails, volume, and music.
